Step 3 of 3 — Bet Amounts

Wolf Denominations

Per-man stakes: the smaller team (2 players, wolf + partner) plays at the small stake, the larger team (3 players, or all 4 opponents on a lone-wolf hole) plays at the big stake. Gross birdies and hammers double the hole's bet.

Trash Denominations

Flat, per trash won — separate from the Wolf hole stakes above, and not doubled by hammers or birdies.

Daytona Denominations

$ per point of differential. Each player on the losing team pays the full point differential individually; the winning team splits that pot evenly.

Match Play is tracked as bragging-rights status only (e.g. "Hardee 2 up") — no $ attached.
